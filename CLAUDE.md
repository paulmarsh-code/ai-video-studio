# AI Video Production Studio: build rules

**Product rule (Paul, D38).** Only add what makes the pictures and clips more consistent and continuous, or makes the finished video better to watch. Before building anything, name:
1. the visible failure it fixes: a picture or clip that came out wrong, or would; and
2. how the fix reaches the generation: the blocking frame, a prompt line, the camera choice, or the picture check.

If it doesn't reach the generation, or nothing visible is wrong, don't build it. Prefer changing an existing rule to adding a new feature, and test against real pictures before adding more.

Standing rules:
- Fixes are general, for any scene and any project; never fitted to one story (Paid On Time is a test case, not the product).
- The app does the heavy lifting from the user's words. Manual controls are for corrections and stay out of the way.
- Every decision goes in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
